About Me

"For beautiful eyes, look for the good in others; for beautiful lips, speak only words of kindness; and for poise, walk with the knowledge that you are never alone." - Audrey Hepburn

Music

My preferred musical genres are Pop and Classical. Currently, "Lovin' It" and "Piano" are playing on repeat. Chopin is my favorite composer–I particularly love Ballade Op. 23 No. 1 and Scherzo Op. 31 No. 2.

Pastimes

In my free time, I enjoy playing piano. Some pieces I've performed include Arabesque No. 2, Rêverie, and Impromptu Op. 90 No. 2. Additionally, I love acting, biking, fencing, practicing calligraphy, and playing card games.

Food

If I could, I'd eat sushi for every meal. My go-to order at Starbucks is a Venti Iced Green Tea Latte, but I appreciate all caffeinated beverages. I'd eat any dessert handed to me, but I especially love tiramisu and bombe Alaska.

Personality

According to 16Personalities, my Myer-Briggs Type Indicator is ENTJ-T and I possess the Commander Personality. I share my personality type with celebrities and historical figures such as Gordon Ramsay, Margaret Thatcher, Bill Gates, Franklin D. Roosevelt, and Steve Jobs, to name a few.

Tech

The text editor I utilize the most is VS Code; the IDE I utilize the most is IntelliJ IDEA; and the programming language I utilize the most is Python. My laptop currently runs Arch Linux, Linux Mint, and Windows operating systems.
